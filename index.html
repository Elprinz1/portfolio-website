04 — About

A data scientist who delivers.

I work where measurement is genuinely hard — marketing ecosystems with no clean control group, products where the metric everyone quotes isn't the one that matters, and LLM systems that look fine until they don't.

Today that means brand relevancy and marketing measurement — the pipelines and statistical models that connect marketing activity to business impact. Along the way I've architected multi-agent GenAI systems, owned product experimentation end to end, and spent the better part of a decade turning customer and operational data into decisions people actually made.

What ties it together is a bias toward the part most people skip: making the answer legible to whoever has to act on it.
